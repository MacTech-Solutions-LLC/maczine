MacZine is the MacTech Solutions article newsletter - written by the
practitioners who run readiness assessments, build CUI enclaves, and carry
authorization packages through assessment for DoD primes and subcontractors.

## What to expect

No press releases, no vendor fluff. Each issue is a working document from
the compliance frontier:

- **CMMC 2.0 and NIST 800-171** - scoping, evidence discipline, and what
  assessors actually ask for
- **RMF and ATO work** - where authorization packages stall and how to keep
  them moving
- **CUI handling** - enclave architecture, marking, and the operational
  realities behind the policy
- **Small-business defense contracting** - compliance economics when you
  don't have a Fortune 500 security budget

## How it works

Every article is written, reviewed, and versioned in the open - the
newsletter is published from a Git repository, reviewed like code, and
deployed like software. That's not a gimmick; it's the same
documentation-as-you-build discipline we push on every engagement.

If there's a topic you want covered, [tell us](/contact). If you're facing
one of these problems right now, [start a readiness conversation](/readiness).

Welcome aboard.
